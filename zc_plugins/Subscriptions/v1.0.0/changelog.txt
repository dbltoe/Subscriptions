Subscriptions -- changelog
==========================

v1.0.0
------
The first release.

- Product setup: a Subscription panel on the product edit page (off,
  optional or required), the intervals offered, a subscribe-and-save
  discount and an optional number of deliveries.
- The storefront choice is a product option the plugin manages
  ("Delivery"), so it works in every template without edits.
- Checkout: a required consent checkbox with your subscription terms,
  saved with each subscription; subscriptions need a customer account;
  a signup email with the terms.
- My Subscriptions in My Account: Renew Now, skip, pause, resume,
  change interval or quantities, cancel.
- Renewals by pay link: an hourly scheduler emails a Renew Now link
  before each renewal; it fills the cart and the customer checks out as
  usual. Nothing is charged automatically. Three missed renewals in a
  row pause the subscription.
- Customers > Subscriptions in the admin: search, filters, history,
  consent record and admin actions, plus the scheduler's status.
- Preview Email definitions for all five emails.
- Runs on Zen Cart v1.5.8 through v3.0.0 and PHP 7.4 through 8.5 from a
  single codebase.
